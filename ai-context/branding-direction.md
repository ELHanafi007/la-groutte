# Branding Direction — La Grotte du Pêcheur

> Dark, cinematic, nightlife-forward. Cave aesthetic meets premium club.

---

## 1. Brand Identity

**La Grotte du Pêcheur** — Seafood Restaurant | Pub | Nightlife Venue
- Open until 4AM
- Live Bands, DJ Parties, Ladies Nights
- Cave/grotto atmosphere with dramatic lighting
- 15.4K Instagram followers

---

## 2. Color Palette — Dark Nightlife

### Primary (Crimson Red — Cave Light)
- `oklch(0.55 0.22 18)` — Deep crimson
- `oklch(0.65 0.22 18)` — Lighter crimson (hover)
- `oklch(0.42 0.22 18)` — Dark crimson (pressed)

### Accent (Amber Gold — Premium)
- `oklch(0.75 0.14 75)` — Warm gold
- `oklch(0.82 0.10 75)` — Light gold
- `oklch(0.62 0.16 75)` — Deep gold

### Backgrounds (Dark Cave)
- `oklch(0.10 0.01 250)` — Deepest black
- `oklch(0.13 0.01 250)` — Surface dark
- `oklch(0.17 0.01 250)` — Elevated surface
- `oklch(0.22 0.01 250)` — Card/modal

### Text
- `oklch(0.95 0.01 80)` — Primary (warm white)
- `oklch(0.70 0.01 60)` — Secondary
- `oklch(0.50 0.01 60)` — Muted

### Usage
- Crimson red → CTAs, accents, event highlights, energy
- Amber gold → VIP, premium elements, subtle warmth
- Dark surfaces → All backgrounds, always dark mode
- This is a **dark-only** design — no light mode

---

## 3. Typography

- **Headlines:** Plus Jakarta Sans (700-800) — bold, modern
- **Body:** Inter (400-500) — clean readability
- **Mono:** JetBrains Mono — data, codes, technical elements

---

## 4. Imagery Mood

- Moody, low-light photography
- Red/amber lighting, cave atmosphere
- Dramatic shadows, cinematic framing
- Smoke, glow effects, nightlife energy
- People enjoying the atmosphere (not posed)

---

## 5. Tone

- Bold, confident, electric
- "The night is yours"
- Not corporate — it's experiential
- Creates desire and FOMO
