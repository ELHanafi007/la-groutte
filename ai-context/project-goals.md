# Project Goals — La Grotte du Pêcheur

> The real project identity and objectives.

---

## 1. Project Identity

**La Grotte du Pêcheur** — A premium seafood restaurant, pub, and nightlife venue.
- Open until 4AM
- Live bands, DJ parties, Ladies Nights
- 15.4K Instagram followers (@la_grotte_du_pecheur)
- Strong nightlife / event culture

---

## 2. The Product

A **smart reservation & QR entry system** that eliminates door friction:
- Customers reserve online → receive a unique QR code
- Doorman scans QR → instant green/red feedback
- No paper lists, no name-checking, no fake screenshots
- Fast, secure, premium, professional

---

## 3. Core Objectives

1. **Zero-friction entry** — QR scan in 0.5 seconds, no questions asked
2. **Anti-fraud** — Cryptographic, single-use, event-bound QR codes
3. **Real-time capacity** — Know exactly how many people are inside
4. **Premium brand presence** — Website that matches the venue's energy
5. **Conversion machine** — Every visitor should want to reserve immediately

---

## 4. Target Users

| User | Needs |
|------|-------|
| **Customers** | Fast reservation, easy QR access, event discovery |
| **Doorman** | Instant scan verification, no ambiguity, works in the dark |
| **Venue Owner** | Capacity control, analytics, no-show tracking, VIP detection |

---

## 5. Success Metrics

| Metric | Target |
|--------|--------|
| Reservation → Check-in rate | > 80% |
| Door scan speed | < 1 second |
| No-show reduction | 50% vs manual |
| Website performance | Lighthouse 95+ |
| Mobile UX | Flawless on all devices |
